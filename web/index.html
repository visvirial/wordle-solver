Wordle Solver

How to use
  1. The solver prefills the active row with its suggested guess. Enter it in your Wordle game, or type a different 5-letter word.
  2. Tell the solver what the game showed: click or tap a tile to cycle gray → yellow → green. Keyboard: 1 gray, 2 yellow, 3 green (fills tiles left to right), ←/→ move the cursor.
  3. Press Submit feedback (or Enter). The remaining candidates and the next suggestion appear.
  4. Code: @ green, + yellow, _ gray — the same notation as the CLI.
